Iron Vault

A community vault for challenge characters. Players who run Hardcore, Ironman or Bloodthirsty share the items they do not need, and others with the same challenges ask for them.

Open the vault Download the addon

What it is

Challenge characters can only trade with characters running the exact same set of challenges. That makes spare cloth, potions, gems and bags hard to pass on, and hard to find when you need them.

Iron Vault is a shared list of what players are willing to give away. You pick your challenges, see what donors with the same set have in stock, and get a ready-made whisper to send them in game. Everything is free.

Taking items

  1. Open the vault and tick the challenges your character runs.
  2. Add what you need to your request with the + button, up to 8 different items.
  3. Enter your character name and press Prepare requests.
  4. Whisper the donor in game with the message you get. If they are offline, send it by in-game mail.

You do not need the addon or an account to take items.

Giving items: install the addon

  1. Download IronVault.zip.
  2. Unzip it into your game folder, under Interface/AddOns. You should end up with Interface/AddOns/IronVault/IronVault.toc.
  3. Restart the game. At the character screen, check that IronVault is ticked under AddOns.
  4. Log in on the character that holds your spare items and open the bank once, so the addon can see it.
  5. Type /ivault. A window opens with your inventory as text: press Ctrl+C.
  6. Paste it in the vault under Share your inventory, untick anything you want to keep, and upload.
  7. Keep the code you receive. It is the only way to update or remove your list.
  8. Keep it fresh without copying. The addon saves your inventory every time you log out. In Chrome or Edge, point the vault at the saved file once (Share your inventory, Automatic updates) and your list refreshes by itself while the page is open.

The addon only reads your bags and bank. It skips soulbound and quest items, and it never moves items, sends mail or presses anything for you. It also notes what you mail or trade away yourself, so requests you fulfilled can be marked as delivered.

Unofficial, player-made

  • Iron Vault is a private project built by another ChromieCraft player. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with the ChromieCraft staff.
  • Trades happen in game, between players. The site only shows lists and prepares messages: it cannot give, take or guarantee anything.
  • Character names are not verified. The public log shows what was asked on the site, not what was actually traded.
  • No account, no email, no payment. The site stores only what you upload: character name, race, challenges, an optional note and the item list.

How this works

Taking items

  1. Tick the challenges your character runs. You only see donors with the exact same set, because only they can trade with you.
  2. Press + (or right click a row) to add an item to your request.
  3. Enter your character name and press Prepare requests.
  4. Whisper each donor the message you get. If they are offline, send it by in-game mail.

Giving items

  1. Install the IronVault addon (unzip it into Interface/AddOns) and type /ivault on the character that holds your spare items.
  2. Copy the text, paste it under Share your inventory and untick anything you want to keep.
  3. Upload. Keep the code you receive: it is the only way to update or remove your list.

Good to know

Everything here is offered for free by other players. Quantities are as fresh as each donor's last upload, shown next to their name.

Ironman characters can equip Poor and Common gear only, so green or better gear is hidden for them.
